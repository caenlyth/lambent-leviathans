# At the Train Station
---
We are all gathered at the [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Millmore Train Station|Millmore Train Station]].

[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Opie|Opie]] enters the train station.

There are members of the [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Watchers Guild|Watchers Guild]] sitting by the door.

[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|I]] enter the station. [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|I]] approach the Ticketmaster. His name is [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Martin Blackwood|Martin Blackwood]], and he wants [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|me]] to put in a good word for him with the [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Observatory|Observatory]]. He doesn't charge [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|me]] for my ticket.

[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Agatha|Agatha]] enters the train station. She is a member of some clergy.

<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Three rugged looking men are present in the station. They are off in the corner having a conversation. These three seem to have a contact on the front dining car of the train. Only Agatha hears this.</mark>

[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Camaria|Camaria]] enters the train station. She has a servant with her.

[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Leaven|Leaven]] enters the train station. The guardsmen glare at him. He knows [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Martin Blackwood|Martin Blackwood]]. He must be a local or spend a decent amount of time in [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Millmore|Millmore]].

There is an elderly lady in a wheelchair, who is accompanied by a heavy set gentleman. She argues with the Conductor before boarding the train. She seems to expect the foremost cars to be reserved for her.

However, some VIPs have reserved the entire front area of the train. Regardless of price, she cannot have the car.

# Aboard the Grounded Zephyr
---
## Main Car
---
[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|I]] explain to [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Leaven|Leaven]] that the [[Lambent Leviathans/Lore/Eldritch Phases|Eldritch Phases]] are random. It is believed that a pattern could be discerned in the future, but for now they are random. They tend to be more common in times of unrest. They are much more common because of the Scourge.

The Conductor passes by. He seems extremely nervous/anxious. There are constant glances to the back of the train.

[[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|I]] inform the guardsmen (Members of the [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Watchers Guild|Watchers Guild]]) that the conductor is exhibiting some strange behavior. The larger one follows after him. The monocled man remains behind.

The Conductor's name is [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucius|Lucius]].

## Agatha's Adventures
---
The wheelchair lady is [[Lady Augustine Treadway|Lady Augustine Treadway]] of [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Auntie Augustine's Apothecary|Auntie Augustine's Apothecary]].

[[Agatha]] meets [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucretia|Lucretia]], a member of the [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Church of Guiding Light|Church of the Guiding Light]]. She is a bishop in the church. She is also blind. She is accompanied by two guards, and seems to either exert her authority or some kind of power to calm down the man named [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Henrick|Henrick]]. After he goes to slap away [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Agatha|Agatha's]] hand. He visibly recoils after [[Lucretia]] speaks his name.

## Arguments in the Rear (Digging)
---
[[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucius|Lucius]] gets into an altercation with a man in one of the rear cars. He seems agitated about checking on some cargo he is meant to deliver. [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucius|Lucius]] informs him that the cargo is safe as the only people who can get into the cargo car are the engineer, security, and [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucius|Lucius]] himself.

[[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Elias|Elias]] (the angry man) becomes increasingly angry despite [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucius|Lucius's]] attempts to qualm him. [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Lucius|Lucius]] eventually relents and agrees to take [[Elias]] back to ensure the safety of the package. Apparently, [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Elias|Elias's]] father would kill him if something happened to the package.

The package supposedly contains some heirlooms/artefacts from [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Weldrake|Weldrake]]. They come from the [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Prism Desert|Prism Desert]]. A long lost fallen empire in the sands.

[[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Francis Green|Francis Green]] is [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Elias|Elias's]] father and the head of the [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Merchants Guild|Merchants Guild]] in [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Cornwall|Cornwall]].

[[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Turner Beckman|Turner Beckman]] is one of [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Elias|Elias's]] companions on the journey. They have been travelling across [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Isle of Lumen|Lumen]]. He appears to be drawing some kind of animal in his journal. It looks like an elk with longer, stringier fur.

There is one other man with [[Elias]] and [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Turner Beckman|Turner]], but he doesn't seem interested in revealing who he is.

## Leaven and Callisto, Super Sleuths
---
[[Leaven]] and [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|I]] make our way towards the front of the train because he gets some kind of itch (location?) and wants to investigate.

We break into the front car just before the Engineer's car. There are a group of men gathered around a table arguing over prices of dwarven ale.

[[Mathias|Mathias]], the proprietor of the [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Tipsy Trout|Tipsy Trout]] (a bar in the [[Lambent Leviathans/Locations/Waterfront District|Waterfront District]]), asks us to settle the argument between him and a man who is clearly a smuggler.

Moving on, we visit the Dwarven engineer in the engine room. His name is [[Lambent Leviathans/NPCs/Clifton Dreyer|Clifton Dreyer]]. He loves this train, which was named by his wife. It was designs and built by the [[Lambent Leviathans/Factions/Visionaries Guild|Visionaries Guild]]. It has dual powered steam engines, with a central T.E.S.L.A coil that transfers power to the pylons on the side of the train. Which send electromagnetic energy to the arcane rails.

## Opie Makes an Entrance
---
[[Leaven]] and [[Lambent Leviathans/Players/Callisto|I]] make our way back to our car. His hunch appears to have been wrong.

After arriving at our car, we see [[Opie]] rise from his seat and begin rushing towards the back of the train. The group gets up to follow after him.

We get to the car where [[Elias]] and [[Turner Beckman|Turner]] were staying. The third man cryptically says that we should come see the [[Lambent Leviathans/Events/Expo|Expo]] and we will know him when we see him.

[[Opie]] makes a grand entrance by ramming through the cargo car door. Inside we see a transformed [[Elias]]. He has gone full beast and is threatening the conductor. There is an automaton in a corner of the car.

The two watchers lay on the ground incapacitated. As we watch, the two watchers begin to rise from the ground and transform.
